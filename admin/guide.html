Your first sign-in

  1. Accept your invitation. Open the invitation email and follow its link to choose your own password.

If you see Netlify’s private-site access screen before the manager, sign in with the Netlify account that has access to this project. This is separate from your website editor sign-in. If you have not received an invitation, ask the website owner to invite your email address.

Social profiles

Change your social, email and support icons

  1. Open Logo, social links & footer and find Social links & icons.
  2. Expand a link to change its Social icon, Link name or Profile URL or email address. Paste a full https:// profile URL, or choose Email and enter your normal email address.
  3. Add a social link to show another profile. Remove a list item to hide its icon, or reorder the list to change the icon order.
  4. For your support link, choose Buy Me a Coffee and enter your direct profile URL, such as https://buymeacoffee.com/mvrkspence. You can edit or remove it in this same list.
  5. Choose Save, then Publish. The icons update on the website and coming-soon page after the website update finishes.

The Contact page uses these shared icons when Use the website social icons is on. For different icons on Contact, open Contact details, switch it off and fill in Custom contact links. Choose an icon and enter a complete https:// profile URL or email address for each link. The contact email and introductory text are edited on that same page.

One photograph, several products

Offer your photograph in different formats

  1. Open Photographs & shop settings. Find Photographs & artwork and choose Add Photograph.
  2. Enter a Photograph reference such as river-photo, its title and description. Upload the photograph in Photograph (upload once from computer). This one upload is reused for every format.
  3. Under Formats & sizes, choose Add Format. Select Art print, T-shirt, Framed print or Canvas.
  4. Give this format a unique Format reference, such as river-print-a3. Enter its Size or option, such as A3, 30 x 40 cm or T-shirt size M, and its Price for this format. Add another format entry for each additional size.
  5. Keep Offer this format on. Leave Enable checkout for this format off for enquiries, or enter a price above zero and switch it on to allow purchases. Keep Test shop with sample products on while practising with example prices.
  6. Save, check the preview and publish. Visitors choose a format and size on the Artwork page before adding that exact product to the basket.

The website uses your photograph to make a visual product preview when a format is selected. This illustrates image placement; you arrange actual printing, production and dispatch yourself. It does not send orders to a printing company automatically.

Switch Offer this format off to withdraw one size, or switch Show this photograph on Artwork off to hide the photograph and all its formats. Keep references unchanged after publishing so baskets and checkout continue to identify the right items.

A separate collection

Add or update a book

  1. Open Books from the dashboard and choose Add Book.
  2. Choose a unique Book reference, enter the Book title and description, and upload the cover. Describe the edition, binding and dimensions where relevant.
  3. Enter Price per book as a number. Books use the currency and flat shipping settings in Photographs & shop settings.
  4. Leave Enable PayPal checkout off for enquiries, or set a price above zero and switch it on for purchases. Keep Show this book on to display it.
  5. Save, check the book preview and publish. Books appear in their own section on the Artwork page.

Photos & lists

Add, replace or remove

Upload a photo from your computer

  1. Open the page or product you want to edit.
  2. Choose the image field, then choose an existing image or upload a file from your computer.
  3. Select the photo and confirm it. Add a short description of what is in the photo.
  4. Check the preview and save your changes.

Manage photos, posts and products

Expand an entry to edit it. Use the list’s add, remove and reorder controls to manage its contents.

Use Show this photograph on Artwork, Offer this format or Show this book to hide items without deleting them. Older standalone products remain under Other / existing products with their Show on Artwork page switch. For a journal post, turn Published on the website off to hide it from the page.

Keep references and permanent IDs unchanged after publishing. Give each photograph, product format, book and journal post its own reference.

When you are ready

Save. Check. Publish.

  1. Save your draft. Your changes stay in the editor until you publish them.
  2. Check the preview. Review your words, pictures and prices. You can return to the draft and keep editing.
  3. Publish the changes. Use the editor’s review and publish controls when you are happy with the result.
  4. View the website. Publishing starts a website update. Allow it to finish, then refresh the website to see the result.

To keep a draft private, leave it in the editor’s draft workflow until you are ready to publish.

A little help

Common questions

Will saving change the website straight away?

No. Save keeps a draft. Publish sends the changes to the website, and the update must finish before visitors see them.

Can I add a piece without selling it yet?

Yes. For a photograph format, leave Enable checkout for this format off. For a book or older standalone product, leave Enable PayPal checkout off. The item shows an enquiry link. To sell it, enter its real price and enable checkout after PayPal has been configured and tested.

Where do I change PayPal?

Open PayPal payments from the dashboard. Paste the Client ID and Secret from the same PayPal app, choose test or real payments, then use Save and connect PayPal. The Secret is stored securely and is not shown again. Leave it blank next time to keep the saved value.

How do I test Visa or debit card payments?

Connect a Sandbox app in PayPal payments and keep the sample shop in test mode. Add an item to the basket and choose Debit or credit card when PayPal makes that option available. Use generated sandbox card details, never a real card. PayPal decides card availability for each buyer.

I published, but I still see the old page.

Allow the website update to finish, then refresh the page. If it still has not changed, ask the website owner to check the Netlify deployment.
