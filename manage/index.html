How to add, replace or remove a picture

Add

Open the page or artwork you want to edit. Use its image field to upload a new picture or choose one from the library.

Replace

Open the existing image field and choose a different picture. Check the preview before publishing.

Remove

Remove the photo entry to take it off a page, or hide the artwork to keep it for later. Save and publish the change.

Removing a picture from a page keeps the file in your library. Delete a library file only when it is no longer used anywhere on your website.
