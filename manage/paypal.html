Test Visa, debit or credit cards

First save the Client ID and matching Secret from your Sandbox app above. Without this connection, the basket cannot start card payments.

Reopen the shop, add a product to the basket and choose Debit or credit card when available. Use PayPal-generated test card details for sandbox purchases. Never enter a real card for a test payment. For the PayPal option, use a separate sandbox buyer account.

PayPal decides which payment options are eligible for each buyer. Connecting the app does not guarantee that a card option appears for every visitor. See PayPal’s payment-button eligibility guidance.
