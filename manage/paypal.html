Shop & payments

Connect PayPal.

Add your payment details, try a test purchase, then switch to real payments when your shop is ready.

Open PayPal payment settings

  1. Get your PayPal app details

    Sign in to the PayPal Developer Dashboard with your PayPal Business account. Open or create a Sandbox app for testing. PayPal shows two details: Client ID and Secret.

    Open PayPal sandbox apps ↗

  2. Add the public Client ID

    Open PayPal payment settings in your editor. Choose Test payments and paste the sandbox Client ID into Test PayPal public client ID. Save your changes, then publish them.

    You can also save a live Client ID for later. The Payment mode choice decides which one the shop uses.

    Edit PayPal payment settings →

  3. Add the secret in secure settings

    Open Netlify’s secure environment settings below. Choose Add a variable, use the test name shown here, and paste your sandbox app’s Secret as its value. Apply it to the production deployment and functions.

    For test payments
    PAYPAL_CLIENT_SECRET_SANDBOX
    For real payments later
    PAYPAL_CLIENT_SECRET_LIVE

    Keep the Secret in Netlify. The editor fields are for public Client IDs; do not paste a Secret or PayPal password there.

    After adding or changing a Secret, open Deploys > Trigger deploy in Netlify. You can also save and publish the PayPal payment settings after adding the Secret. The website needs a fresh deployment to use the new value.

    Open secure Netlify payment settings ↗

Try a test purchase

Allow the website update to finish. Add a sample product to the basket, open PayPal and pay using a separate sandbox buyer account. Sandbox payments use test money.

Keep Test shop with sample products on while using the example catalogue. The website will reject live payments while that switch is on.

When you are ready

Switch to real payments

Replace all sample products and prices with your real details. Get your live app’s Client ID and Secret from PayPal, save the Client ID in the editor and the matching Secret in Netlify using the live name above. Turn off Test shop with sample products, choose Real payments in PayPal payment settings, and publish your changes after the Secret is saved so a new deployment starts.

Keep the shop private until you have checked checkout and fulfillment for the products you intend to sell.
